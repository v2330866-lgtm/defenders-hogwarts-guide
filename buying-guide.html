02 · Problems

Order problem or missing component?

Before contacting support

Check components against printed page 4 of the rulebook. Prepare your order reference, a clear description, and photos of any damage. The FAQ asks for damage/defect reports within 7 days of receipt.

Returns & cancellation

The campaign cancellation cutoff (September 30, 2026) has passed. Unopened base-game returns: 28-day window with a 20% administration fee; delivery charges excluded. Read the full policy first.

Who to contact: campaign backers use the official support-ticket system; general product questions go to MinaLima's contact page. Keep personal order details out of public comments.
